Privacy Policy — Delivery Performance & Operations Dashboard

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Overview

This privacy policy explains how the BREADFAST — Delivery Performance & Operations Dashboard Chrome Extension handles information when used by authorized users with Breadfast operational systems.

The extension is designed for operational monitoring and analytics, including delivery activity, branch performance, workforce visibility, fleet and trip activity, zone analysis, and historical operational reporting.

2. Information handled

The extension may process information needed to provide its operational functionality, including:

  • Operational order and delivery information.
  • Workforce information such as picker and delivery-associate names, identifiers, status, and performance-related records.
  • Branch, zone, trip, fleet, route, and operational activity information.
  • Information contained in authorized Breadfast operational pages and network responses used by the dashboard.
  • Authentication-related request context required to access authorized Breadfast operational systems.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide and improve the extension's stated operational functions, including:

  • Displaying live operational dashboards and monitoring views.
  • Calculating and presenting operational performance indicators and trends.
  • Supporting branch, workforce, zone, delivery, fleet, and trip monitoring.
  • Supporting historical comparisons and dashboard continuity.

4. Storage and retention

Some dashboard settings, caches, and operational data may be stored locally in the user's browser to support continuity and historical dashboard features. Local retention is limited to what is needed by the extension's operational functionality and configuration.

5. Sharing and third parties

The extension does not sell user data or use it for advertising. Data is not transferred to third parties except where necessary to provide the extension's stated functionality.

The extension communicates with authorized Breadfast operational systems. When route distance or duration calculations are required, relevant route information may be processed through Google Maps Platform / Routes services for that specific operational purpose.

6. Authentication and security

The extension relies on the user's authorized access to Breadfast operational systems. Authentication information is used only to communicate with those authorized systems and is not used for unrelated tracking or advertising.

7. Data use limitations

We do not sell user data. We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single stated purpose, and we do not use or transfer user data for creditworthiness or lending purposes.
